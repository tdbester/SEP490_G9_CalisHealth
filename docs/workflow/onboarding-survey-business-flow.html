CalisHealth — Onboarding Survey & Training Plan Recommendation

The whole journey in one picture: how the Content Manager shapes the onboarding survey, how a new member walks through it, and how the answers turn into a fitness level plus a training plan they can preview and start. Everything below is traced straight from the current source code.

How to read it

Three lanes, one story. The Content Manager lane is where the survey is designed, the CalisHealth lane is the quiet machinery that keeps everything honest, and the New Member lane is what a person actually experiences.

Part 1 — The survey gets built

The Content Manager opens Survey Management and works on the live survey version. Every new step is created as a draft, so nothing reaches a member until it is deliberately switched on. While the step is being written, CalisHealth quietly checks that every question type is understood, that a question is not used in two steps at once, and that a "show this only if…" condition can only look backwards at questions the member has already answered. If a change would break the survey, the whole save is rejected and the manager is told what to fix — the survey is never left in a half-changed state.

A big part of the work is the preview: the manager sees the step exactly as a member will see it, and can type in test answers to see how the validation reacts. Nothing is stored, no session is created — it is a rehearsal. Finally the step is switched on, and the Result step is configured with the shape of the plan that will be recommended later: how many weeks, how many sessions a week, how long each session lasts, whether to show the score breakdown and the plan preview, and which level to fall back on if the member never finishes the level test.

Part 2 — The member answers

A member who signs in for the first time is guided straight into the onboarding survey. CalisHealth opens a session for them and pins it to the survey version that is live right now, so the questions never shift under their feet halfway through. If they leave and come back, they continue the same session with their earlier answers intact.

Each step is saved all-or-nothing: the answers are checked against the real rules first — right type, inside the allowed range, required questions filled in, follow-up questions matching the earlier choices — and if anything is off, the member is told exactly what to correct and nothing is written. When they tap Continue on the final step, the survey is marked complete and the level is scored in the same breath.

Members are never trapped. "Skip for now" keeps everything they have entered, drops them back on the dashboard, and a reminder later invites them to finish it.

Part 3 — Result, plan and training

The result screen shows the level they landed on, the score of every move in the level test, and the plan that goes with it. The plan comes from the training templates: the one built for their level and, when possible, for the exact number of days a week they said they want to train — otherwise the closest match.

"Preview training plan" opens a month calendar with the training days marked and the exercises of the selected day, so they can see the shape of the weeks before committing. "Start my training plan" confirms it, writes the level and the weekly rhythm onto their profile, and returns them to the dashboard to begin training. And if the level does not feel right, they can go back to the level test and redo it — the old score is cleared and replaced, never stacked.
